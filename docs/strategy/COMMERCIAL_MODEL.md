# Valoris Commercial Strategy & Revenue Model
**Project:** Valoris (Internal Codename: ODRM-Copilot)
**Theme:** Waste to Wealth (Blue Ocean Strategy)

## 1. Executive Summary
Valoris is a B2B SaaS platform that acts as the **decision-making layer** for flare gas valorization. We provide the "Shovel and Picks" (AI-driven chemical simulation and economic analysis) so operators can maximize the ROI of their physical infrastructure. We are the **Action Layer** for the Nigerian Oil and Gas Industry Emission Tracker (NOGIET).

## 2. The Market Gap (Blue Ocean)
*   **The Old Way:** Multi-million dollar proprietary software (Aspen HYSYS) requiring PhD-level chemical engineers to operate. Slow, inaccessible, and cost-prohibitive.
*   **The New Way (Valoris):** A rapid, AI-powered surrogate model backed by a Techno-Economic Analysis (TEA) engine. Fast (milliseconds), cheap (SaaS subscription), and accessible to any plant operator. We don't just detect the problem (like NOGIET); we prove the solution.

## 3. The 3-Phase Go-To-Market Strategy
Instead of a standard SaaS launch, we are executing a phased public-private partnership strategy targeting the Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) and the Global South.

### Phase 1: The Pilot Integration Mandate (Current)
*   **Target:** NUPRC and 3 selected NGFCP operators.
*   **Offer:** Train our AI on their real-world historical data and provide a Regulatory Dashboard to track compliance.
*   **Revenue:** A Pilot Mandate Grant (non-dilutive funding).
*   **Purpose:** Prove technical feasibility on real-world "dirty gas" and establish Valoris as the regulatory standard.

### Phase 2: The National Integration (Post-Pilot)
*   **Target:** NUPRC (National Rollout).
*   **Offer:** Full integration into the NOGIET platform. Onboarding of all 28 NGFCP operators.
*   **Revenue:** Annual government licensing fee + B2B SaaS subscriptions from the operators.
*   **Purpose:** Deep lock-in. When NOGIET detects a plume, Valoris is the native tool that shows the financial solution.

### Phase 3: Operational Intelligence & Cryptographic ESG
*   **Target:** International ESG Investors & Global South Operators.
*   **Offer:** Predictive Maintenance and Tamper-Proof Carbon Credits.
*   **Revenue:** Premium SaaS tier + transaction fees on verified carbon credits.
*   **Purpose:** Global expansion and capturing the high-margin international ESG market.
