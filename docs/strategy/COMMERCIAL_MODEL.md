# Valoris Commercial Strategy & Revenue Model
**Project:** Valoris (Internal Codename: ODRM-Copilot)
**Theme:** Waste to Wealth (Blue Ocean Strategy)

## 1. Executive Summary
The global energy transition is heavily focused on hardware (building physical reactors to capture flare gas). However, hardware is expensive, slow to deploy, and often inaccessible to mid-sized operators in the Global South. Valoris is a B2B SaaS platform that acts as the **decision-making layer** for flare gas valorization. We provide the "Shovel and Picks" (AI-driven chemical simulation and economic analysis) so operators can maximize the ROI of their physical infrastructure.

## 2. The Market Gap (Blue Ocean)
*   **The Old Way:** Multi-million dollar proprietary software (Aspen HYSYS) requiring PhD-level chemical engineers to operate. Slow, inaccessible, and cost-prohibitive.
*   **The New Way (Valoris):** A rapid, AI-powered surrogate model backed by a Techno-Economic Analysis (TEA) engine. Fast (milliseconds), cheap (SaaS subscription), and accessible to any plant operator.

## 3. Revenue Model (How We Make Money)
Valoris employs a multi-tiered B2B SaaS revenue model. Customers are paying for the *decision support*, not just the raw prediction.

### A. The "Explorer" Tier (Freemium / Low Barrier)
*   **Target:** Junior engineers, students, and small-scale operators.
*   **Offer:** Limited access to the Surrogate AI API. Single-reactor simulation without TEA or Dashboard access.
*   **Price:** Free.
*   **Purpose:** Customer acquisition and establishing Valoris as the industry standard.

### B. The "Operator" Tier (Core SaaS)
*   **Target:** Mid-sized flare gas operators in the Global South (Nigeria, Angola, etc.).
*   **Offer:** Full access to the **ESG Dashboard** and **TEA Engine**. Unlimited predictions, real-time ROI calculations, carbon credit estimations, and downloadable ESG compliance reports. 
*   **Price:** $X,000 / month per facility.
*   **Purpose:** Recurring, high-margin revenue. They pay for the ability to prove profitability and ESG compliance to their investors.

### C. The "Enterprise" Tier (Custom / API Integration)
*   **Target:** Major oil & gas companies and hardware manufacturers (e.g., ESCo2).
*   **Offer:** Direct API integration into their proprietary plant control systems. Custom TEA models trained on their specific regional gas prices and carbon tax liabilities.
*   **Price:** Custom licensing + retainer.
*   **Purpose:** Deep lock-in. Once their plant dashboard relies on our TEA Engine, switching costs are incredibly high.

## 4. The "Shovel and Picks" Philosophy
We do not build or sell physical reactors. We sell the intelligence that makes the reactors profitable. By staying hardware-agnostic, we can partner with *every* hardware manufacturer in the market. If they sell a reactor, they can bundle a Valoris subscription with it because we provide the financial justification (TEA) and the ESG report the end-user needs to secure funding.

## 5. Go-To-Market Strategy
*   **Phase 1 (Validation):** Open-source the synthetic data generator and core validator under the MIT License (already done in Sprint 1) to build technical credibility and establish Valoris as the industry standard.
*   **Phase 2 (Beta):** Partner with 2-3 mid-sized Nigerian operators to test the Valoris API against their historical flare data.
*   **Phase 3 (Scale):** Launch the SaaS platform globally, targeting the ESG reporting requirements of international companies operating in the Global South.
